PROBLEM STATEMENT
 

Traditional artisans possess valuable skills, cultural knowledge, and unique handmade products, but many face barriers when attempting to access digital marketplaces.

These barriers include:

* Language and English-literacy barriers
* Limited digital literacy
* Difficulty creating professional product catalogues
* Difficulty typing product descriptions
* Limited access to wider markets
* Dependence on intermediaries
* Difficulty understanding digital commerce processes
* Challenges with product pricing and market information

KarigarSetu addresses these challenges through a voice-first, AI-powered mobile platform designed around the needs of artisans.

OUR VISION
KarigarSetu is an AI-powered, voice-first platform that helps marginalized artisans create digital product catalogues and connect with wider markets.


#WHAT IS KARIGARSETU??

KarigarSetu is an AI-powered, voice-first platform that helps marginalized artisans create digital product catalogues and connect with wider markets.

Instead of requiring artisans to type complicated English descriptions, they can simply speak about their product in their preferred regional language

AI assists with:

* Speech recognition
* Language understanding
* Translation
* Product information extraction
* Catalogue generation
* Product description creation
* Price-range assistance
* GST/HSN guidance
* Voice-based product search
 
The artisan can review, repeat, correct, and confirm the generated information before publishing it.

KEY FEATURES :
# 🎙️  1.  Voice-First Interaction  - Artisans can describe their products through voice instead of typing.

* Reduces dependence on typing
* Helps users with limited digital literacy
* Makes digital platforms easier to access
* Allows artisans to communicate naturally

  
# 🌐 2. Regional Language Support - Artisans can interact with the system using their preferred regional language.

*This reduces the language barrier that can prevent artisans from participating in digital commerce.
*The artisan's lack of English proficiency should not become a barrier to showcasing their craftsmanship.


# 🤖 3. AI-Powered Smart Cataloguing - AI converts the artisan's spoken information into structured product information and generates a professional product description.

Artisans can create digital catalogues without needing:
* English writing skills
* Professional copywriting
* Extensive typing
* External assistance
 This can reduce one source of dependence on intermediaries.

# 🔁 4. Repeat & Correct Mechanism - The artisan can repeat their input whenever the system does not understand them correctly.

This keeps the artisan in control of the final information instead of making the AI's first output final.


# 🛍️ 5. Digital Product Catalogue

The platform can generate structured listings containing:
* Product name
* Description
* Material
* Colour
* Size
* Craft category
* Images
* Price
* HSN information
* GST information where applicable
It helps artisans present their products in a standardized digital format suitable for online discovery.


# 💰 6. AI-Assisted Price Range - KarigarSetu can provide an estimated price range based on available product and market information.

Possible factors include:
* Material
* Labour
* Craft complexity
* Production time
* Product characteristics
* Market listings where available
THE ARTISAN RETAIN CONTROL OVER THE FINAL PRICE.
This can help artisans make more informed pricing decisions and reduce the risk of undervaluing their work.
